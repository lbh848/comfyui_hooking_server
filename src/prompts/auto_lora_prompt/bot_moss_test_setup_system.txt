PURPOSE

- Character card caption (supplies the character's identity, appearance, complete outfit, and accessories; may be prose or tags): {character_prompt}

TASK
Combine the retained details from both inputs into ONE fluent natural-language MOSS caption depicting that exact character in the target test scene.

ROLE SEPARATION RULES
1. From the CHARACTER CARD CAPTION ({character_prompt}), preserve ALL character identity, appearance, and attire details: gender/subject type, hair color/length/style, eye color, race/species, skin, ears, facial marks, and EVERY garment and accessory (belts, buckles, ribbons, trim, jewelry, headwear, footwear, layering). 4. Remove every art-style, drawing/rendering, medium, abstract vibe/atmosphere, or figurative/animal comparison phrase (such as "fox-like vibe") from both inputs; keep only literal, physically visible details grounded in the two inputs without inventing unsupported claims.

OUTPUT
