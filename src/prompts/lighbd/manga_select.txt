PURPOSE

MANGA-CHARACTER-RESOLVE resolves registered CURRENT participants and selects connected source passages to recap as manga pages inserted immediately after those passages. Apply the shared CHARACTER-RESOLVE identity rules to the same roster, card references, persona and CURRENT context; earlier history and saved state supply continuity only. Keep all registered CURRENT participants in the top-level characters and only each page's selected visible subjects in scene_plan.characters.

Each requested output image is ONE manga page. The requested page count is independent of the number of panels. Select the requested number of page insertion anchors using the existing characters + scene_plan output contract. Selection has the same responsibility whether PLAN is enabled or disabled: identify participants, source ranges and concise scene intent. Leave panel count and layout to the downstream page-planning stage; do not select or compress a passage to fit a preferred number of panels. PLAN, when enabled, expands that intent before DETAIL. With PLAN disabled, DETAIL performs the expansion itself. Do not output panels, dialogue, geometry, canvas sizes or image prompts.

SOURCE-FAITHFUL SELECTION

Choose a coherent event range that preserves its visible cause, action and source-established consequence or reaction. Let the event determine the range, without assuming a panel count. Read the whole source context semantically; do not map one sentence to one panel. In scene_brief, identify the passage's beginning and ending C anchors and summarize the event to preserve. Set anchor_segment to the end of that passage so the page recaps preceding text. Do not include future events after that insertion anchor. Earlier history supplies continuity, not new recap material.

Prioritize the initiating action, central event and its source-established result. Include setting or an intermediate step only when needed to understand the relation. A source with no completed result remains unfinished. Never invent a punchline, reversal, resolution, prop or accident to fill a four-part story template. Narrative roles help choose meaningful beats; they are not mandatory labels or output fields. Omit an intermediate action only when the remaining pictures can supply its causal link. Preserve essential objects, contact changes, directed attention and visible reactions that make that link readable.

When the passage contains one short event, choose materially distinct supported moments or views of its action and reaction. Do not fill a panel with an invisible internal state or invent a new event. Across requested pages, select different source-supported event ranges without repeating the same recap merely to meet the count.

Distinguish emotional atmosphere and figurative narration from physical scene facts before writing scene_brief. Translate a dark mood, cold hostility or a heavy atmosphere only into source-supported visible expression, gaze, gesture or posture. Do not carry metaphorical darkness, coldness, murk or pressure into the brief as lighting, weather, colored effects or environmental conditions. Preserve the continuing physical setting and illumination established in the surrounding source even when the selected passage describes only a reaction; a mood change does not dim a sunlit room. Darkness inside a separately enclosed space does not darken the room around it.

SCENE PRIORITY

Select the coherent range that gives the requested page its strongest source-supported story beat. When the requested page count cannot cover every event, prefer the range containing the defining visible action and its source-established consequence over a range containing only setup, anticipation or aftermath. A page may include surrounding steps when they make the central event readable; do not let them displace the event itself.

When the supplied TRUSTED ACTIVE BOT IMAGE POLICY or explicit-scene guidance authorizes explicit content and CURRENT contains a sexual or NSFW event, select a range that can recap that event coherently: the acting instant and the source-established response or consequence that belongs to it. If the range contains penetration or creampie, include the act itself so the page-planning stage can allocate the required junction panel; do not select only its approach or aftermath. Do not choose a page that stops at the approach, conceals the act behind a reaction shot, or skips directly to the aftermath. Preserve the event's supported intensity, exposure, contact and participant direction. Under a single-subject policy, choose the range and views that remain readable with the permitted partner fragment; never widen the partner's visibility to make the page easier.

ACTOR AND TARGET HINTS

Identify the acting and receiving sides of the page's central physical action from the source itself, never from sex, gender, grammatical subject, name order, registration or array order. A directed action has an actor and a target; a mutual action has equal endpoints and no target; a solo or independent action has no target. Keep that direction unchanged across the selected range and state it in scene_brief so the page-planning stage can preserve it. Do not swap roles between panels unless the source explicitly shows that reversal. The hint never adds identities to scene_plan.characters, changes the registered array or overrides the visible-subject policy.

IDENTITY AND VISIBILITY

Keep the active bot policy and the supplied character limit. Under SINGLE, the named LoRA subject remains the sole main subject in every panel. An interaction partner remains off-frame or the smallest connected anonymous fragment required for comprehension: no identifiable face, full silhouette, separate identity entry or added person-count tag. Select a recap that remains readable under this policy. Do not reveal the partner more widely to make the sequence easier.

OUTPUT

Use the existing characters and scene_plan response. For each page, return only its end anchor_segment, selected canonical characters and one concise scene_brief identifying the source span and event to preserve. Write scene_brief and all descriptive prose entirely in English, even when the source is Korean. Summarize source speech in English when relevant instead of copying Korean dialogue into the brief. Only dialogue text in the downstream drawing stage uses the requested dialogue language; this selection stage outputs no dialogue. Keep canonical machine identifiers unchanged in their identifier fields. Do not expand the event into a panel-by-panel script. Preserve the requested page count and chronological order.

FINAL CHECK

Requested page count, chronological order, exact end anchors and distinct event ranges. If an authorized explicit event is selected, its range contains the act itself and its source-established response or consequence, not only setup or aftermath. Confirm that actor and target keep their source-supported direction across the page. Keep each scene_brief to one concise sentence.
