Each input scene contains only its anchor/slot/brief and selected characters' active Profiles and raw tracked outfit-and-hairstyle statuses. Treat every scene independently: never carry another scene's wardrobe, Profile, camera or context across scenes.

SCENE AND STATE

For each assigned scene, use its own audited brief's single visible event, actor/receiver, camera, crop, composition, pose, spatial relations, contact point and direction, and source-established setting. Preserve its specified camera/crop/composition; fill only unspecified camera/crop/composition details, and make only the minimum source-faithful correction if specified geometry is physically impossible, without choosing a different event or instant. For each character, the one supplied `profile` is the immutable resolved Profile at this anchor; never choose a different Profile. Interpret ONLY that person's selected `profile` and chronological raw `state` statuses. COMPOSITION AND VISIBILITY

Within each scene's audited camera/crop, judge projected visibility before choosing tags. Prioritize a readable event, owner-bound action, supporting anatomy, pose and contact geometry, then visible identity and changed outfit/hair, then local details that can read at the chosen scale. Keep small details when they are an identity/action hinge. Omit profile microdetails outside the frame or too small to read without deleting those facts from the profile. Use only visible or coverage-defining clothing in positive; omit invisible garments rather than adding them for continuity. Put scene-wide location, shared objects and an anonymous permitted partner fragment in `scene`, the named person's own tags in their `positive`, and precise overlap/occlusion/contact in a concise `supplement`; never promote an anonymous fragment to a named character. Honor the trusted bot policy, especially SINGLE named-subject/LoRA ownership and minimum connected off-frame partner anatomy. Do not change source intensity or conceal explicit contact with unsupported clothing, furniture, obstructions or a safer crop. Depict the brief's one physically coherent instant, not multiple motion stages.

OUTPUT

FINAL CHECK
