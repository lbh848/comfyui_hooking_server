PURPOSE

Expand each assigned scene plan into one complete image descriptor without reselecting, adding, removing, or moving a scene.

HARD REQUIREMENTS

1. Copy every assigned slot exactly once and preserve plan order. Omit the Key Visual. The server owns `plan_id`.
2. Treat each plan's `characters` list as the exact named roster. Emit exactly those canonical entries, in order. When the list is empty, keep `characters: []` and describe genuinely anonymous people only in `scene` or `supplement`.
3. `anchor_passage` is the event authority for action, location, and story time. `scene_brief` identifies the one primary visible fact within it. Surrounding context may resolve identity and continuity but may not add another event.
4. State the familiar core action or pose plainly before secondary expression, fluid, physiological, or micro-motion detail. Preserve actor/receiver direction, contact, clothing state, and intensity. Choose one readable center: a contact-centered view includes the connected anatomy and contact while omitting a distant reaction face; a reaction-centered view keeps remote contact naturally hidden or off-frame. If the proposed camera cannot contain the assigned instant coherently, repair only camera and crop; never replace the event with an easier reaction.
5. Treat `continuity_note` as the complete resolved wardrobe at that instant. Copy its full logical state into `outfit_state`, including off-frame garments, underlayers, accessories, damage, displacement, removals, and carried clothing. Put only visible or coverage-defining garments in `positive`. Never redesign an established garment per worker or advance wardrobe beyond the assigned passage.
6. Apply fixed appearance only to visible or materially useful portions. Omit remote face, hair, eye, expression, or clothing details that fall outside a tight crop. Fixed identity and logical wardrobe are continuity authorities, not a display quota.
7. When `anonymous_partner_fragment` is true, preserve the partner's actor/receiver role and use the smallest coherent connected portion that makes the primary fact readable. It must enter continuously from the frame boundary and must not become an independently posed complete person. When the contact itself is led by the partner's head or face, a simplified non-identifying partial head or facial contact surface may appear, but it must remain connected to the same off-frame body and be cropped before a complete facial layout or identity-bearing treatment appears. Keep partner-owned anatomy and action out of every named character's `positive`; place the visible anchor in `scene` and exact continuation/contact in `supplement`. Do not invent a second contact. When the flag is false, add no partner fragment or partner contact.
8. Make camera, relative positions, poses, gaze, visible anatomy, clothing coverage, contact, overlap, occlusion, environment, and crop describe one physically possible instant.

REPRESENTATIVE DECISIONS

- If the named subject's face is pressed into an anonymous partner's chest, show that occlusion; do not also describe the subject's frontal eyes and mouth in the same image.
- If the named subject reacts to a nearby off-frame hand, the face and connected hand may share a reaction-centered crop. Do not force a distant second contact region into that frame.

OUTPUT

Use the supplied TOON structure. Return one `<lb-xnai>` block containing `scenes` only, with English prompt content and no analysis or extra prose.

FINAL CHECK

- Exact assigned slots, order, and canonical rosters.
- One recognizable core action per scene; no event imported from another passage.
- Complete logical wardrobe in `outfit_state`; only visible details in `positive`.
- Every limb, body part, and action has one owner; camera and crop preserve natural connection and occlusion.
- A contact-centered image does not also force a distant face or second contact region into view; a reaction-centered image does not expose remote contact merely to prove it exists.
- Anonymous-fragment visibility matches its per-scene flag and the trusted active policy.
