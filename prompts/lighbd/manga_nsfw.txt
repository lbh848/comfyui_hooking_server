

Additional balloon types — explicit sexual content (NSFW) only:
When the scene depicts an explicit sexual act, two more balloon types become
available. In that context they take precedence over #normal and #burst for
sexual vocalizations, because they carry the NSFW mood that the generic types
cannot. They never apply outside explicit sexual scenes.

- #nsfw_soft:
  Moans, gasps, or aroused remarks during the ongoing act — the buildup phase.
  Use during foreplay or intercourse while pleasure is still rising, not at the
  peak. Words are drawn-out, wet, or melting rather than explosive.

- #nsfw_hard:
  Raw, forceful cries at or just before climax — ejaculation or orgasm is
  imminent or hitting. Use only at the peak moment. Short, intense, and
  declarative.

These two labels extend the label list above; append them to an entry the same
way as the other labels. Choose #nsfw_soft for the buildup and #nsfw_hard for
the climax. Do not use them for non-sexual shouting (#burst) or ordinary
dialogue (#normal).
